A) About - top

The main task of this plugin is send you `Elementor Pro Form Widget - Bitrix24 CRM - Integration` forms directly to your Bitrix24 account.

B) Step 1 - Settings in CRM - top

Go to your `Bitrix24` -> `Applications` -> `Web hooks`.
https://your_bitrix24/marketplace/hook/

Click `ADD WEB HOOK`. Choose `Inbound web hook`.

Check `Tasks`, `Tasks (extended permissions)`, `CRM` and `Chat and Notifications (im)`. Click the button `SAVE`.

Copy value from `REST call example URL` without `profile/`.

C) Step 2 - Integration - top

Go to the `Elementor` -> `Bitrix24`

Insert in the field `Inbound web hook` copied value.

Validate webhook.

D) Step 3 - Form action after submit - top

Open to edit the page with the form that you want to send to the Bitrix24.

Click on the form and click on section `Actions After Submit` on the left. Click in the `Add action` field and select the type you need "Lead, Deal, Task, Company or Contact".

After selecting a section will appear with fields to fill.

Below is an example of the filled-in fields.

Fill fields and click "Update" button.

When sending the form, the data will be transferred to Bitrix24.

E) Additional information - top

Where can I find the shortcode field to insert?

Open section `Form Fields`, click on the desired field, switch to the tab `Advanced`. In the field `Shortcode` there is a shortcode that can be inserted into the fields for the Bitrix24.

Additional shortcodes

  • [utm_medium]
